## 2. How each feature works

### Vehicles (`shared/m3.js`, `server/rooms/CityRoom.js`, `src/economy.js`, `src/jobs.js`, `src/main.js`)
- Buy at the **Showroom** POI (within 300 px). Cash first, then Bank, never negative (same rule as the hospital fee). The vehicle is parked on the open lot **south** of the Showroom (`y + 270`) — the building itself is blocked, so parking at the POI would strand it where nobody can mount it (found by the test run, fixed in both server and offline economy).
- `E` (or the Lên xe button) mounts the nearest owned vehicle within 80 px. Speed applies to movement (`vehicleSpeed` × `speedWithHunger`, sprint on foot only) and to Shipper deadlines: **deadline = distance / vehicle speed + 30s, locked at accept**; reward uses the locked multiplier of the vehicle ridden at accept. Changing vehicle mid-order does not change the deadline.
- Motorbike drains fuel (100% = 8.000 px); bicycle drains durability (100% = 50.000 px). At 0 the vehicle is pushed at walk speed (180 px/s) and the player is told `out_of_fuel` / `worn_out`. Refuel (5.000đ) / repair (10.000đ) at the shop, within 300 px.
- Shipper capacity (10/13/18 kg) is defined but **not enforced** — the P0 order pool has no weight field.

### Taxi (`shared/m3.js` §3.4.2, `server/rooms/CityRoom.js`, `src/jobs.js`)
- Job `taxi_driver` + **motorbike required** (bicycle cannot carry a passenger). "Nhận khách" toggles taxi mode. NPC customers wait at sidewalk corners of road intersections and appear only when < 4 real players are in the room (max 4 customers, despawn after 5 min).
- Real players: driver invites a nearby player → they accept → ride. Dropoff (button, or driver dismounts) completes the trip.
- Fare = 1.000đ/100 px travelled, clamped 5.000–20.000đ. **Tax 5% (LOCKED, buyer pays):** passenger pays fare + 5%; driver receives fare + night bonus (SYSTEM, +30% capped at 6.000đ). If the passenger cannot pay in full, the driver gets only what was actually paid — no SYSTEM subsidy of the missing fare (same never-negative rule as the hospital fee). **NPC passenger fare is paid by SYSTEM** (the NPC has no wallet) — driver still gets fare + night bonus.
- Offline: same engine with local NPC customers (fare paid by SYSTEM, `economy.grant('taxi_fare')`).

### Doctor mini-game (`src/jobs.js`, `index.html` #revivePanel, server revive)
- Clicking Hồi sinh on a fainted player opens the rhythm panel: 10 beats × 1.100 ms, tap when the marker is inside the green zone (42–58%). Accuracy ≥ 90% → victim gets 30 + 20 = 50 HP; below that, 30 HP. The client-sent accuracy is clamped 0–100 and the +20 bonus is computed **server-side** (`M3.doctorMinigameBonus`) — the client cannot forge it. Reward (25.000đ), 60s cooldown and the 2/60min pair cap are unchanged.

### Online self-faint SFX fix (known M3 gap)
- The server now broadcasts an explicit **`fainted`** message `{playerId, name, x, y, at}` to the whole room when a player faints (in addition to the doctor-only `faint_alert`). The client plays `DTA_SFX.faint` from `onFainted` for self, plus the `syncSelf` isFainted edge as fallback; both funnel through a 3-second throttle so one faint never plays twice. No new SFX methods were added — M4 reuses existing motifs.

### Motel (`shared/m3.js` §8.2, `server/rooms/CityRoom.js`, `src/economy.js`)
- Rent 50.000đ / 24h real at the Motel POI (within 300 px); renew extends **from the old expiry** (not from now), max 7 days ahead. Room gives a 10-slot chest.
- Offline: chest lives in the same `localStorage` wallet as everything else (`dta_m2_wallet_v1`). Online: per-session server state keyed by `sessionId` — a reconnect gets a new session, so vehicles/motel do **not** survive a rejoin yet (Supabase assets wiring is M2/M5 work).
- Unpaid rent → room repossessed: chest state `held` for 24h after expiry (locked until rent is paid), then `deleted` (items gone).
